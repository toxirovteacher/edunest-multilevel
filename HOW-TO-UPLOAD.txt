EDUNEST - Multi-Level upload: the new Grammar page, 20 Nov 2026
================================================================

WHAT IS NEW
-----------
Grammar topics now have their own page, grammar.html. The first topic on it is
"Past Simple, Would - Used to". It has two parts:

1. DARS (the lesson): your rule, split into 10 short cards that the student goes through one by one
   ("Keyingi" / "Oldingi" buttons, or swipe on the phone).
   - The rule text is YOUR text from the PDF: all of it, word for word, in the same order.
     The build stops with an error if any line of your rule is missing, repeated or moved.
   - What WE added around your text, so you can check it:
       * colours on your formula lines (Subject / did / Verb 2 / object), the words are yours
       * 5 small time-line pictures (bir marta / takror-takror / endi yo'q)
       * 5 short "Diqqat" notes (e.g. "did dan keyin doim Verb 1")
       * a table of the 14 irregular verbs that this topic uses (buy - bought, see - saw ...)
       * "Tekshirib ko'ring": 14 quick questions, 1-2 at the end of every card, with a short
         explanation (practice only: no score, nothing is sent to you)
       * titles on the 6 cards that have no heading in your text (e.g. "So'roq shakli", "Inkor shakli")
       * the last card: a table "Past Simple, used to yoki would?" and all the formulas together
   - Big-screen mode: on a laptop or PC there is a monitor button at the top right. It makes the
     lesson large for the classroom screen (the arrow keys change cards). Phones do not show it.

2. MASHQLAR (exercises): the same 5 exercises as before (10 + 8 + 8 + 8 + 6 = 40 questions),
   now ONE question at a time. Each answer is checked at once and the student sees the right answer
   and the Uzbek explanation. At the end: the score, the list of mistakes with explanations, and
   "Xatolar ustida ishlash" (do only the wrong ones again; the score stays the first try's score).

THE MAP, "BAJARDIM" AND XP: same as before
  - Lesson step: "Bajardim" appears after the last card. Students who try to finish in under
    1 minute are asked to read first.
  - Exercise step: "Bajardim" appears on the result page; you see "7/10 to'g'ri" etc. as before.
  - If you ALREADY published "Grammatika · Past Simple, Would - Used to": you do NOT need to publish
    it again. The old links open the new page by themselves. Students who already did some
    exercises keep their answers.
  - In "Tayyor materiallar" the steps are now: "Dars: Past Simple, Would - Used to" + Mashq 1-5.


HOW TO UPLOAD (repository: toxirovteacher/edunest-multilevel), ONE commit
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Open the repository on GitHub -> "Add file" -> "Upload files".
2. Open the folder "edunest-multilevel" (next to this file). Select everything inside it (Ctrl+A)
   and drag it onto the GitHub page. That is 8 files:
     gr1.bin, grammar.html, index.html, mashq.html,
     css/grammar.css, js/app.js, js/grammar.js, js/mashq.js
   Drag what is INSIDE "edunest-multilevel", not the folder itself.
   GitHub will say that some files replace old ones: that is correct.
3. Press "Commit changes".
4. After 1-2 minutes open the site and press Ctrl+F5.
5. Tell me "uploaded" and I will check the live site.

Only the Multi-Level site changes. General-3 and IELTS: nothing to upload from this folder.
(The IELTS folder EDUNEST-UPLOAD-IELTS-20NOV is a separate upload and still waits for you.)
