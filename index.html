  1. Open the booklet flat at pages 10–11. All 8 black corner squares should be in the photo (or 4, if you scan one page at a time).

  3. Read the handwritten name shown on screen, type it once, then Save.
  4. One page at a time also works: scan page 10, then page 11 of the same student.
